## Getting Started

### Creating Your Account

#### For Parents

1. **Access the Registration Page**
   - Navigate to the application URL
   - Click "Register" or "Sign Up"
   - Select "Parent Account"

2. **Fill in Your Information**
   - **Name**: Enter your full name (alphabetic characters only)
     - ✅ Valid: "John Smith", "Mary Anne"
     - ❌ Invalid: "John123", "Mary@Smith"

   - **Email**: Provide a valid email address
     - This will be used for account recovery
     - ✅ Valid: "parent@example.com"
     - ❌ Invalid: "parent@", "notanemail"

   - **Password**: Create a strong password
     - Minimum 8 characters
     - Must include at least one uppercase letter (A-Z)
     - Must include at least one lowercase letter (a-z)
     - Must include at least one special character (!@#$%^&*)
     - ✅ Example: "MyChild2024!"
     - ❌ Example: "password" (too weak)

   - **Confirm Password**: Re-enter your password

3. **Complete Registration**
   - Click "Register" button
   - You'll be redirected to the login page
   - Use your email and password to log in

#### For Administrators

1. **Access Administrator Registration**
   - Navigate to registration page
   - Select "Administrator Account"

2. **Enter Admin Passkey**
   - You'll need the administrator passkey
   - Default passkey: `child1234` (check with your organization)
   - This provides an extra security layer

3. **Complete Registration**
   - Follow the same steps as parent registration
   - All password requirements apply

### Logging In

1. Navigate to the login page
2. Enter your registered email address
3. Enter your password
4. Click "Login"
5. You'll be directed to your dashboard

### Forgot Password?

1. **Request Password Reset**
   - Click "Forgot Password?" on login page
   - Enter your registered email address
   - Click "Send Reset Link"

2. **Check Your Email**
   - You'll receive an email with a reset link
   - The link is valid for 1 hour only
   - Check your spam folder if you don't see it

3. **Reset Your Password**
   - Click the link in the email
   - Enter your new password
   - Confirm the new password
   - Click "Reset Password"
   - Log in with your new credentials

## Parent Account Guide

### Managing Your Profile

#### Viewing Your Profile
1. Click on "Profile" in the navigation menu
2. You'll see:
   - Your personal information
   - List of your children
   - Custom tests you've created

#### Editing Your Information
1. In the Profile page, find the "Edit Profile" section
2. Update your name or email
3. Click "Save Changes"

#### Changing Your Password
1. Go to Profile page
2. Find "Change Password" section
3. Enter:
   - Current password
   - New password (must meet strength requirements)
   - Confirm new password
4. Click "Change Password"

### Managing Children Profiles

#### Adding a Child

1. **Navigate to Add Child**
   - Go to Profile page
   - Click "Add Child Profile" button

2. **Fill in Child Information**
   - **Name**: Child's full name
   - **Date of Birth**: Select from date picker
   - **Age**: Current age in years
   - **Grade Level**: Current educational level
     - Examples: "Preschool", "Kindergarten 1", "Kindergarten 2"
   - **Gender**: Select Male, Female, or Other
   - **Notes** (optional): Any additional information about your child

3. **Save Profile**
   - Click "Add Child"
   - The child will appear in your children list

#### Editing a Child Profile

1. Go to Profile page
2. Find the child in your children list
3. Click "Edit" button next to the child's name
4. Update any information
5. Click "Save Changes"

#### Deleting a Child Profile

⚠️ **Warning**: This action cannot be undone and will delete all associated data.

1. Go to Profile page
2. Find the child in your children list
3. Click "Delete" button
4. Confirm the deletion

#### Selecting a Child to View

1. **From Dashboard**
   - If you have multiple children, you'll see a selection screen
   - Click on the child's card to select them

2. **Switching Between Children**
   - Click "Select Child" in the navigation
   - Choose a different child
   - All data will update to show the selected child's information

## Module-Specific Guides

### 1. Preschool Milestone Tracker

**Purpose**: Track your child's developmental achievements across various domains.

#### Adding a Milestone

1. **Navigate to Preschool Tracker**
   - Select your child
   - Click "Preschool Tracker" in the menu

2. **Click "Add Milestone"**

3. **Select Developmental Domain**
   - Physical Development
   - Cognitive Development
   - Social-Emotional Development
   - Language Development
   - Motor Skills (Fine/Gross)

4. **Describe the Milestone**
   - Be specific about what your child achieved
   - Examples:
     - "Can count from 1 to 10 independently"
     - "Successfully ties shoelaces without help"
     - "Shares toys with other children willingly"
     - "Recognizes and names primary colors"

5. **Set the Date**
   - Choose the month and year when this milestone was achieved
   - The system will calculate your child's age in months at that time

6. **Save the Milestone**
   - Click "Add Milestone"
   - View the milestone in your list

#### Understanding AI Analysis

After adding milestones, the system will automatically:
- Compare your child's achievements to age-based benchmarks
- Identify areas that are:
  - **Age-appropriate**: Developing as expected
  - **Advanced**: Ahead of typical development
  - **Delayed**: May need additional support
- Provide an overall development summary

#### Best Practices
- Record milestones promptly when you notice them
- Be honest and accurate in your descriptions
- Add milestones regularly for comprehensive tracking
- Review the AI analysis to understand your child's development patterns

### 2. Academic Progress Tracker

**Purpose**: Monitor your child's performance in various subjects over time.

#### Adding Academic Records

1. **Navigate to Academic Progress**
   - Select your child
   - Click "Academic Progress" in the menu

2. **Click "Add Record"**

3. **Enter Record Details**
   - **Subject**: Choose from dropdown or enter custom subject
     - Pre-defined: Math, English, Science, Bahasa Malaysia
     - Custom: Enter "Other" and specify

   - **Score**: Enter the score (0-100)

   - **Date**: Select month and year
     - Records are organized by month
     - Choose when the score was achieved

4. **Save the Record**
   - Click "Add Record"
   - View the updated chart

#### Interpreting the Progress Chart

- **X-axis**: Time (months/years)
- **Y-axis**: Scores (0-100)
- **Lines**: Each subject has its own colored line
- **Trends**:
  - Upward trend = improvement
  - Downward trend = may need attention
  - Flat trend = consistent performance

#### Viewing Insights

The AI Insights module will analyze academic data to:
- Identify strong subjects
- Highlight areas needing support
- Suggest home-based activities
- Provide encouragement and guidance

### 3. Learning Style Analyzer

**Purpose**: Identify how your child learns best through observations and assessments.

#### Part A: Adding Observations

1. **Navigate to Learning Style**
   - Select your child
   - Click "Learning Style" in the menu

2. **Record Observations**
   - Click "Add Observation"
   - Describe what you've noticed about your child's learning preferences

3. **Examples of Good Observations**
   - "Prefers looking at picture books over listening to audiobooks"
   - "Learns letters better when tracing them with fingers"
   - "Remembers songs and rhymes easily after hearing them once"
   - "Enjoys building blocks and hands-on activities"
   - "Asks many questions and likes detailed explanations"
   - "Focuses better when moving around rather than sitting still"

4. **Save Observation**
   - Click "Add Observation"
   - It will appear in your observations list

#### Part B: Creating Custom Tests

1. **Navigate to Profile**
   - Go to Profile page
   - Find "My Tests" section

2. **Create New Test**
   - Click "Add New Test"
   - Enter test name
     - Examples: "Visual Learning Assessment", "Reading Preferences", "Activity Preferences"

3. **Add Questions**
   - Click "Add Question"
   - Enter your question
   - Choose answer type:
     - **Text**: Open-ended response
     - **Scale**: 1-5 rating (1=Strongly Disagree, 5=Strongly Agree)

4. **Example Questions**

   **For Scale Type (1-5)**:
   - "My child prefers learning with pictures and diagrams"
   - "My child remembers things better when they hear them"
   - "My child likes to touch and handle objects while learning"
   - "My child enjoys reading books independently"

   **For Text Type**:
   - "What activities does your child enjoy most?"
   - "How does your child typically react to new information?"
   - "Describe a situation where your child learned something quickly"

5. **Save Test**
   - Click "Save Test"
   - Test is now available to use

#### Part C: Taking Tests

1. **Navigate to Learning Style**
   - Select your child
   - Click "Learning Style" in the menu

2. **Select Test**
   - Find "Take a Test" section
   - Choose test from dropdown

3. **Answer Questions**
   - The test questions will load
   - For scale questions: Select 1-5 rating
   - For text questions: Type your response

4. **Submit Test**
   - Click "Submit Answers"
   - Responses are saved

#### Understanding Learning Style Results

The AI will analyze all observations and test responses to identify your child's learning style:

- **Visual Learners**: Learn best through seeing
  - Prefer pictures, diagrams, colors
  - Remember faces better than names
  - Like to watch demonstrations

- **Auditory Learners**: Learn best through hearing
  - Enjoy discussions and oral instructions
  - Remember what they hear
  - Like music and rhymes

- **Reading/Writing Learners**: Learn best through text
  - Prefer reading and writing activities
  - Take notes and make lists
  - Like books and written instructions

- **Kinesthetic Learners**: Learn best through doing
  - Hands-on activities are preferred
  - Like to move and touch
  - Learn by practicing

- **Mixed Learners**: Combination of styles
  - Benefit from varied teaching methods
  - Flexible learning preferences

The analysis will also provide:
- Reasoning for the identified style
- 3-5 practical suggestions for parents
- Activity recommendations

### 4. Tutoring Recommendations

**Purpose**: Get personalized suggestions for additional support based on comprehensive child data.

#### Accessing Recommendations

1. **Prerequisites**
   - Complete preschool milestone tracking
   - Add learning style observations and/or tests
   - The more data, the better the recommendations

2. **Navigate to Tutoring**
   - Select your child
   - Click "Tutoring Recommendations" in the menu

3. **Review Recommendations**
   The system analyzes:
   - Developmental areas needing support
   - Learning style preferences
   - Academic performance patterns

4. **Generated Recommendations Include**:
   - Specific subjects or skills to focus on
   - Teaching approaches aligned with learning style
   - Types of activities that would help
   - Frequency and duration suggestions
   - Whether external tutoring might be beneficial

#### Regenerating Recommendations

1. Click "Regenerate" button
2. System will create fresh recommendations based on latest data
3. Review updated suggestions

### 5. Personalized Learning Plan

**Purpose**: Receive a structured weekly activity schedule tailored to your child.

#### Accessing Your Learning Plan

1. **Prerequisites**
   - Have some academic records
   - Complete learning style analysis
   - Add preschool milestones (if applicable)

2. **Navigate to Learning Plan**
   - Select your child
   - Click "Learning Plan" in the menu

#### Understanding the Plan

The learning plan includes:

1. **Learning Plan Summary**
   - Overview of your child's current status
   - What to focus on this week
   - Encouragement for parents

2. **Weekly Action Plan**
   - Structured schedule Monday through Sunday
   - 2-4 activities per day
   - Each activity is 10-20 minutes
   - Activities are age-appropriate and aligned with learning style

3. **Long-Term Strategy**
   - Habits to build
   - Routines to establish
   - Ways to support ongoing development

#### Implementing the Plan

**Tips for Success**:
- Don't try to do everything at once
- Start with 1-2 activities per day
- Be consistent
- Make it fun, not a chore
- Adjust based on your child's mood and energy
- Celebrate small achievements

**Example Daily Routine**:
```
Morning (10 min): Reading activity
After lunch (15 min): Hands-on math game
Evening (10 min): Creative expression
```

## Tips and Best Practices

### Data Entry Best Practices

1. **Be Consistent**
   - Record data regularly (e.g., weekly)
   - Use similar formats for descriptions
   - Keep dates accurate

2. **Be Specific**
   - Instead of "Did well in math" → "Correctly solved 8/10 addition problems (numbers 1-20)"
   - Instead of "Talked well" → "Used complete sentences with 5-7 words consistently"

3. **Be Honest**
   - The AI provides better insights with accurate data
   - Don't inflate scores or achievements
   - Record both strengths and challenges

### Using AI Features Effectively

1. **Provide Sufficient Data**
   - More observations = better analysis
   - Multiple test responses improve accuracy
   - Regular updates lead to better trends

2. **Regenerate When Needed**
   - After adding significant new data
   - When you notice major changes in your child
   - Monthly refresh recommended

3. **Interpret with Context**
   - AI suggestions are guidance, not rigid rules
   - Consider your child's unique circumstances
   - Combine AI insights with your parental knowledge

### Privacy and Security

1. **Protect Your Account**
   - Use a strong, unique password
   - Don't share your login credentials
   - Log out on shared devices

2. **Child Information**
   - Only enter necessary information
   - Keep notes professional and factual
   - Remember data is stored digitally

3. **Email Security**
   - Use a secure email account
   - Keep your email password safe
   - Don't share password reset links

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General Questions

**Q: Can I track multiple children?**
A: Yes! You can add as many child profiles as needed. Switch between them using the "Select Child" feature.

**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: Yes. The application uses industry-standard security practices including password hashing, secure sessions, and data validation.

**Q: Can I export my data?**
A: Currently, data export is not available but is planned for future updates.

**Q: How often should I update information?**
A: For best results:
- Milestones: As they occur
- Academic records: Monthly or after assessments
- Observations: Weekly
- AI insights: Regenerate monthly

### Technical Questions

**Q: The AI analysis isn't generating. What's wrong?**
A: This could be due to:
- Insufficient data (add more milestones/observations)
- API quota limits (wait and try again later)
- Network issues (check your connection)

**Q: I forgot my password. What do I do?**
A: Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page. You'll receive a reset email (valid for 1 hour).

**Q: Can I change my child's information later?**
A: Yes, you can edit child profiles anytime from the Profile page.

**Q: What if I enter data incorrectly?**
A: You can delete individual records (milestones, academic scores, observations) and add correct ones.

### Feature Questions

**Q: What's the difference between observations and tests in Learning Style Analyzer?**
A:
- **Observations**: Your notes about what you've noticed
- **Tests**: Structured questionnaires with specific questions

Both are used together for comprehensive analysis.

**Q: How accurate are the AI recommendations?**
A: AI suggestions are based on educational research and data patterns. They provide helpful guidance but should be combined with:
- Your parental knowledge
- Professional educator input (if available)
- Your child's individual needs and circumstances

**Q: Can teachers or other family members access the account?**
A: Currently, each account is individual. Consider sharing insights verbally or through screenshots if needed.

**Q: What age range is this suitable for?**
A: The application is designed for preschool-age children (typically 3-6 years old), but can be adapted for slightly older or younger children.

### Data Questions

**Q: How far back can I add data?**
A: You can backdate entries to any historical date. This is useful for:
- Recording past milestones you remember
- Entering previous assessment scores
- Building a comprehensive history

**Q: Will old data affect current recommendations?**
A: Yes, the system considers historical patterns to provide contextual insights and identify trends.

**Q: Can I delete data?**
A: Yes, you can delete:
- Individual milestones
- Academic records
- Observations
- Test answers
- Entire child profiles (with confirmation)
